1. CRITICAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Qubit One is ONE connected Enterprise Operating System.

It contains multiple business domains, but those domains are NOT independent applications.

Existing domains include:

- Home
- My Workspace
- Enterprise
- People / HR
- Sales
- Finance
- Inventory
- Marketing
- Service
- Meetings & Collaboration
- AI
- Analytics
- Security
- Administration

These domains are separated internally for architecture and maintainability.

However, the user experience must remain connected.

Example:

A Sales Executive working inside Sales may have authorized access to:

- Mail
- Meetings
- Calendar
- Tasks
- Customers
- CRM
- Sales AI
- Approvals
- Notifications
- My HR information

without needing to treat each capability as a completely separate application.

Therefore:

MODULE STRUCTURE ≠ USER EXPERIENCE STRUCTURE.

Do not turn Qubit One into a collection of disconnected portals.

- deterministic where possible
- idempotent
- transactional where appropriate
- observable
- auditable
- permission-aware
- retry-safe
- failure-aware

≠
Email delivered.

Create payment request
≠
Payment completed.

Create dispatch
≠
